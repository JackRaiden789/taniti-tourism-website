About Taniti

A quick introduction to help you decide what to explore during your visit.

Taniti is an island destination with a mix of tropical beaches, rainforest, ocean activities, and local culture. Visitors can spend time around Yellow Leaf Bay, explore hiking opportunities in the island’s lush interior, or learn more about the island through tours and cultural attractions.

The island also has a small, active volcano in its mountainous interior, so visitors should check local guidance and access conditions before planning a trip there.

Helpful details before departure

Visitor Information

Keep practical visitor information handy as you plan your stay on Taniti.

$

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Some businesses also accept euros and yen. Banks facilitate currency exchange, and many businesses accept major credit cards.

ϟ

Power outlets

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.

◷

National holidays

Some attractions and restaurants may close on national holidays, so plan ahead.

＋

Safety & healthcare

Be aware of your surroundings and take normal precautions against petty theft. The island has a hospital and several clinics.

A

Language

Many younger residents speak fluent English. Less English is spoken in rural areas, especially by older residents.

✈

Arrival

Most visitors arrive by air. A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.
